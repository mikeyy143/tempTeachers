About the APTET Key Score Calculator

The APTET (Andhra Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test) Answer Key Score Calculator is a dedicated utility for teacher aspirants across Andhra Pradesh. It automates the evaluation of official APTET response sheets released by the School Education Department, Government of Andhra Pradesh.

How APTET Marks are Evaluated

The APTET examination consists of 150 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) carrying 1 mark each, with a total duration of 2.5 hours. Key highlights of the marking structure include:

  • Positive Marks: +1 mark for every correct answer.
  • Negative Marking: No negative marking for wrong or unattempted answers.
  • Total Score: Calculated as the sum of correct answers out of 150 marks.
